    paragraphs:
    - "Sárvár is a spa town in western Hungary, known for its healing thermal water. Today it offers much more than a few days in the bath: wellness, active holidays, nature and history, from the Nádasdy Castle to the arboretum."
    - "The town’s tourism destination management office (TDM), which also runs the local Tourinform office, operates sarvar.hu for visitors from Hungary and abroad."
-
    id: "goal"
    title: "The goal"
    paragraphs:
    - "The site has to inspire people to come, and then help them plan the stay. It had four jobs:"
    list:
    - "Show everything the town offers beyond the spa"
    - "Help visitors plan their trip: sights, events and accommodation"
    - "Speak to visitors in their own language"
    - "Work for everyone, whatever device or assistive technology they use"
-
    id: "accessibility"
    title: "Accessible by design"
    paragraphs:
    - "Accessibility was part of the work from the start. According to the site’s accessibility statement, it aims to meet the WCAG 2.1 AA standard:"
    list:
    - "The page can be zoomed up to 400% without the text overflowing the screen"
    - "The whole site can be used with a keyboard or a screen reader, and it was tested with NVDA"
    - "If animations are turned off in the operating system, they don’t run on the site either"
    - "A contrast mode can be switched on right in the header"
