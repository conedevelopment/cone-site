    paragraphs:
    - "la Blanka started in 2019 on the main square of Biatorbágy. After years of experimenting, Blanka, who is coeliac herself, finished baking school, while Dani set up their first shop."
    - "Their flour mixes are made from wholegrain, slow-release gluten-free flours, and they are also dairy-free, egg-free, soy-free, vegan and free of additives. They sell them online and through about two dozen shops and cafés across Hungary."
-
    id: "goal"
    title: "The goal"
    paragraphs:
    - "Gluten-free shoppers read before they buy: the ingredients, the nutrition facts and everything else the product is free from. The new site had to put those answers first."
    - "It had three jobs:"
    list:
    - "Sell the flour mixes, with every detail a coeliac shopper checks before buying"
    - "Make ordering simple for Hungarian shoppers"
    - "Show where the mixes can be bought offline"
-
    id: "shop"
    title: "The shop"
    paragraphs:
    - "The products run on WooCommerce with our own templates. Every mix comes in a 1 kg and a 5 kg pack, and its page lists the ingredients, the nutrition facts per 100 g, the dietary labels and the customer reviews."
    - "Recipes sit alongside the products as inspiration, and each one links to the mix it uses."
    - "The checkout is set up for Hungarian shoppers:"
    list:
    - "Invoicing through Számlázz.hu"
    - "Delivery by GLS courier or to a GLS parcel point"
    - "Payment by bank transfer or cash on delivery"
